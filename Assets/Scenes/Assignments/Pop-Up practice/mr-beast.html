    About

    He originally gained widespread popularity there for his video series Worst Intros on YouTube, as well as his frequent gameplay videos. He later began filming videos involving generous financial prizes. As an investor and an entrepreneur, he founded the delivery-only fast food company MrBeast Burger and he has invested in several tech companies.  He also launched a successful chocolate bar company called Feastables.  In June of 2025 he became the first individual creator to surpass 400 million YouTube subscribers.

    Before fame

    He began his YouTube channel on February 19, 2012. He posted his first video there the next day.

    Trivia

    He has earned 470 million subscribers to his MrBeast YouTube channel. He has five additional YouTube channels called MrBeast Gaming, MrBeast 2, Beast Philanthropy, Beast Reacts, and MrBeast 3. He was named Creator of the Year at the 2020, 2021, 2022, and 2023 Streamy Awards. In November of 2022, he surpassed T-Series and PewDiePie to become the most subscribed creator on YouTube.  He released Beast Games on Amazon in December of 2024.

    Family life

    He was born and raised in Wichita, Kansas. His real name is James Stephen Donaldson. He featured his mom in his YouTube video titled "Surprising My Mom With $10,000+ Home Makeover." His older brother CJ Donaldson has a popular YouTube channel called MrBro. He previously dated Maddy Spidell. In 2022, he started dating Thea Booysen, and the couple got engaged on Christmas day in 2024.

    Associated with

    During the 2016 election season, he made multiple comedic videos concerning Donald Trump.
